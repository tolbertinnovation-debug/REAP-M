- DM Sans and DM Serif Display are self-hosted from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the fonts in `public/assets/`.
- Lucide icons are provided by the `lucide-react` package under its ISC licence.
- `mark.svg` is an original simple leaf mark authored for this interface.
